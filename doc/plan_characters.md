## **Characters, Tribe-Inspired Costumes, and Buffs**

* **Standard Urban Character:** Has no special buff.  
* **Ata of Paquibato:** The Ata are an Indigenous people of Davao City, including Paquibato. [Wycliffe Philippines](https://www.wycliffephilippines.org/projects-atatribe?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  
  * **Weapon — Basket:** Captures an enemy so the player can throw it and deal damage.  
  * **Costume color buffs:**  
    * **White:** Dodges all attacks for 3 seconds.  
    * **Black:** Reflects damage back at the attacker.  
    * **Red:** Increases attack damage.  
    * **Blue:** Restores HP.  
    * **Yellow — “Promise”:** Buff effect to be decided.  
* **Bagobo-Tagabawa of Binugao:** SunStar identifies BIBATAWA as the Binugao Bagobo-Tagabawa Women’s Association. [sunstar.com.ph](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/davao/bibatawa-keeps-tribal-heritage-alive-in-davao?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  
  * **Weapon — Needle and Thread:** Delivers quick, repeated pokes.  
  * **Special ability — Thread Snare:** Uses thread to immobilize an enemy temporarily. This ability has a cooldown.

## **General Weapons and Buffs**

* **Durian:** Throw it to make it explode on impact and remove 1 heart from an enemy, or consume it to restore 1 player heart. Durians can be stacked in the inventory.  
* **Cacao Beans:** Increase weapon attack speed for 3 seconds.  
* **Kadayawan Combo (Tentative):** Collecting or harvesting multiple buffs increases the score multiplier.  
* **Agila — Philippine Eagle:** Slows the player’s fall. Can be used once.  
* **Honey:** Slows an enemy’s movement for 3 seconds. Can be used once.

## **Enemies/Boss**

* **Rotten Cacao — Levels 1–5:** Has 3 hearts. Its poison removes 1 player heart every 10 seconds, so the player must defeat it before the next poison tick.  
* **Evil Durian — Levels 1–5:** Has 3 hearts. Its spike attack removes 1 player heart and knocks the player backward when it hits.  
* **Spanish Colonizer/Soldier — Level 2:** A friar briefly obscures the player’s vision for 3 seconds, then disappears for 1 second.  
* **Japanese Colonizer/Soldier — Levels 3–4:** At long range, throws sushi projectiles in a 3-second attack that repeats every 2 seconds. At close range, attacks the player with a wooden stick.  
* **American Colonizer/Soldier — Level 4:** At long range, throws books in a 3-second attack that repeats every 2 seconds. At close range, punches the player.
