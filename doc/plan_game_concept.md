**Game Name Suggestion:**

- Davao Chronicles: Through The Ages  
- Davao Run: A Dash through History  
- Davao Quest: Echoes of History

## **Game Concept**

**Platform:** Godot  
**Art style:** 32-bit-style pixel art  
**Due date:** Within three weeks

### **Main Goal**

Make learning about Davao’s history interactive and fun.

### **Main Game Mechanics**

* At the start menu, players can choose a level and a character wearing a tribe-inspired costume.  
* Each character costume provides different buffs.  
* Each level has its own difficulty and covers a different period in Davao’s history.  
* When players hover over a level title in the selection menu, a short description of that historical period appears.  
* At the beginning of each level, its date and title appear at the top center of the screen for three seconds. The title then becomes smaller and stays visible throughout the level.  
  *Example: “1700s – Pre-Colonial Davao”*  
* Players avoid obstacles, collect cacao to earn points and increase their score multiplier, collect power-ups to help fight the boss, and reach the end of the level.  
* Obstacles may include blocks or other objects, platforms to jump across, gaps in the ground, and enemies.  
* Each player starts with three hearts. The player can lose by falling through a gap or losing all their hearts to enemies.  
* Enemies have three to five hearts, depending on the level’s difficulty. Players must collect weapons and buffs to defeat them.  
* Each level should take an average of at least one minute to complete, including time spent avoiding obstacles and fighting the boss.

### **Losing and Winning**

* **When the player loses:** Show **“Maayo ang pagsulay”** (“Nice try”) with **“Don’t give up.”** underneath. A button then returns the player to the start menu.  
* **When the player wins:** Show **“Larga na, Davao\!”** (“Let’s go, Davao\!”). A button then returns the player to the start menu so another player can take a turn.

**NUMBER OF LEVELS** 

* **Level 1 \- Trade, Tides & Traditions:** Davao Before Colonization  
* **Level 2 \- New Flags, New Rules:** The Spanish Conquest  
* **Level 3 \- Abaca Boom, City Bloom:** Japanese Arrival and Davao’s Growth  
* **Level 4 \- Davao Under Fire:** World War II  
* **Level 5 \- Old Roots, New Routes:** Modern Davao

**SCORE (TENTATIVE)**

- cacao points (score multiplier)  
- getting weapons/buffs (including costume buff) also score multiplier(TENTATIVE)  
- getting all cacao and defeating the boss on the specific level will give 3 stars/(or an element of davao)

- example:   
- 15 cacao \= 3 stars,   
- 10-14 cacao \= 2 stars,   
- 9-5 cacao \= 1 star,   
- less than 5 cacao, 0 stars.
