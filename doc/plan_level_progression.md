
## **Progressive Stages For The Game:**

### **Stage 1:** The Ancient Trading Gulf

**Historical basis:** Trade among Indigenous communities and maritime traders before colonization.

**Possible game elements:** Rivers, coastal routes, traditional settlements, and collectible trade goods.

**Setting**: Ancient Davao gulf, rivers, forests, and indigenous trading settlements.

**Possible Obstacles & Enemies:** 

Floating Driftwood: Logs float towards the player and must be jumped over.

Strong River Currents: Push the player backward, slowing movement.

Falling Durian: Drop from trees at random intervals.

Mangrove Roots: roots that block the path, requiring jumping or sliding.

Broken Bamboo Bridges: collapse shortly after being stepped on.

Moving Trading Boats: Boats cross the player's path, requiring precise timing.

Muddy Riverbanks: Reduce movement speed and jumping distance.

Thorny Forest Vines: Damage players who touch them.

Special obstacle: Rising Tide — Water will rise gradually.

### **Stage 2:** The Spanish Expedition

**Historical basis:** The 1848 expedition led by José Cruz de Uyanguren and Datu Bago's resistance.

**Possible game elements:** A historical setting inspired by Bolton Riverside and the Davao Gulf. The stage should portray the conflict respectfully.

**Setting:** Davao's riverside settlements during the Spanish expedition of 1848 and resistance associated with Datu Bago.

**Possible Obstacles & Enemies:**

Rolling Cannonballs: Roll across the ground and must be jumped over.

Cannon Fire: Projectiles follow a predictable arc, requiring dodging.

Wooden Barricades: Block pathways and can be destroyed using weapons.

Falling Fort Debris: Wooden beams fall from damaged structures.

Spike Barricades: Require jumping or finding an alternative path.

Guard Patrols: Colonial soldiers patrol designated areas and can be avoided or defeated.

Crumbling Watchtowers: Platforms collapse after the player crosses them.

River Crossing: Players must jump between moving boats to reach the opposite bank.

Special obstacle: Cannon Barrage — Multiple cannons fire in alternating patterns while the player crosses a fortified area.

### **Stage 3:** The Plantation Era

**Historical basis:** The growth of abaca plantations, Japanese migration, and the development of Mintal as “Little Tokyo.”

**Possible game elements:** Abaca farms, plantation landscapes, and agricultural collectibles.

**Setting:** Abaca plantations, agricultural processing facilities, and the developing communities of early 20th-century Davao**.**

**Possible Obstacles & Enemies:**

Abaca Fiber Bundles: Large bundles obstruct the path and require jumping.

Rolling Abaca Carts: Move horizontally toward the player.

Falling Wooden Crates: Drop from storage platforms.

Tangled Abaca Fibers: Slow the player when stepped on.

Plantation Irrigation: Ditches Gaps requiring accurate jumps.

Moving Farm Machinery: Rotating mechanisms require timed movement.

Unstable Wooden Walkways: Collapse after a short delay.

Muddy Plantation Paths: Reduce traction and movement speed.

### **Stage 4:** World War II and Liberation

**Historical basis:** The Japanese invasion in 1941, the Davao Dozen escape in 1943, and the liberation campaigns of 1945.m

**Possible game elements:** A wartime environment, obstacles inspired by the historical setting, and a mission based on escape or survival.

**Setting:** Wartime Davao, including jungle escape routes, damaged roads, and occupied areas.

**Possible Obstacles & Enemies:**

Barbed Wire Fences: Must be jumped over or avoided.

Falling Building Debris: Rubble falls from damaged structures.

Exploding Shells: Marked ground areas explode after a warning.

Spotlights: Players must hide behind objects to avoid detection.

Landmines: Hazardous tiles require careful navigation.

Collapsed Bridges: Require jumping across broken sections.

Military Roadblocks: Block paths and require detours or destruction.

Enemy Patrols Soldiers follow patrol routes, requiring stealth or combat.

Smoke Clouds Temporarily obscure the player's vision.

Crumbling Trenches Players must jump over gaps in damaged defensive positions.

### **Stage 5:** A Changing Davao

**Historical basis:** The division of Davao Province in 1967 and the creation of Davao de Oro and Davao Occidental.

**Possible game elements:** A map showing the changing provinces and the growth of local communities.

**Historical basis:** Davao's Indigenous and Moro communities, festivals, traditional crafts, and agricultural industries.

**Setting:** Modern Davao City, featuring urban development, agriculture, cultural heritage, and transportation**.**

**Possible Obstacles & Enemies:**

Moving Jeepneys: Vehicles cross the player's path at varying speeds.

Road Construction Barriers: Require jumping or sliding.

Falling Durian Fruits: drop from trees and must be dodged.

Rolling Fruit Carts: Move across market pathways.

Flooded Streets: Slow movement and may hide small hazards.

Open Drainage Canals: Require precise jumps.

Hanging Electrical Wires: Low obstacles requiring players to slide underneath.

Construction Debris: Falling materials create temporary hazards.

Market Crates: Stacked crates form climbing and jumping challenges.

Busy Intersections: Players must time crossings between moving vehicles.

Special obstacle: City Rush — A fast-paced section combining jeepneys, construction barriers, and market carts, testing the player's timing and reaction speed.

**Suggested obstacle progression:**

To make the game feel increasingly challenging, each level should introduce a new gameplay mechanic.

Level Main mechanic   
1  Ancient Trading Gulf: Jumping and avoiding natural hazards  
**Difficulty:** Easy

2  Spanish Conquest: Dodging projectiles and destroying barricades   
**Difficulty:** Easy–Medium

3  Plantation Era: Moving obstacles and timed platforms   
**Difficulty:** Medium

4 World War II: Stealth, projectiles, and environmental hazards   
**Difficulty:** Hard

5  Modern Davao: Combined mechanics and faster movement   
**Difficulty:** Very Hard
